CSV Formatting Guide

The importer auto-detects two formats:

  • Standard: Date, Type (Deposit/Withdrawal), Category, Amount, Description.
  • Bank Export: Date, Description, and either a single 'Amount' column OR split 'Debit' and 'Credit' columns.

The parser automatically ignores bank preambles (junk text at the top of the file) and normalizes messy dates.
